## Validation Strategy (Pre-Launch)

### Organic > Paid Ads
- Organic traffic = stronger validation signal
- People searching have real intent (actively looking for solutions)
- Paid traffic often just curiosity clicks
- Organic compounds over time; paid disappears when you stop spending

### The Playbook
1. **Build organic distribution BEFORE the full product**
2. Create landing page explaining problem + solution
3. Add email signup for early access
4. Focus on getting organic traffic to validate interest

### SEO Foundation (Weeks 1-8 Timeline)
- Week 1-2: Submit to 200+ startup/product directories for domain authority
- Week 3: Search Console shows increased crawling
- Week 3+: Publish 3+ blog posts targeting problem-based searches (helpful, not promotional)
- Week 4-8: Traffic builds, domain authority grows
- Result: 500 organic visitors, 40 email signups = real validation

### Validation Conversations
- Talk to signups (aim for 15+ conversations)
- Learn: features they need, pricing expectations, alternatives tried
- These people actively searched = high-quality feedback

## Core Philosophy

### Open Source Strategy
```
Open source the sexy experiments
Monetize the boring problems
Free builds credibility
Paid solves real pain
```

### The Real Problem Approach
**Stop chasing AI trends. Build for timeless problems:**
- People want to look good
- People want to make money
- People want to be productive
- People want to remember things
- People want to eat better
- People want better relationships

**AI is just the tool. The problem is timeless.**

Nobody pays for "AI features" - they pay to fix their actual life.

**Formula:** Pick a real problem + Add AI to solve it faster

## TokenTrail Positioning

### The Timeless Problem We Solve
- Businesses need to track and bill for API usage (existed before AI)
- Multi-tenant SaaS needs usage metering
